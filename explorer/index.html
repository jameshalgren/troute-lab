About. Generated from the reproducible experiment repro/network_stats/ (NWM v3.1.6 RouteLink) and the NWM v3 hydrofabric geodatabase; see explorer/DATA_CONTRACT.md. A segment starts a new reach unless it has exactly one upstream segment. CONUS feature IDs link to the USGS NLDI record (NHDPlus V2 COMID); Hawaii and Puerto Rico IDs are NHDPlus V2.1 COMIDs and Alaska IDs NHDPlus HR NHDPlusIDs (or Arc-Hydro segments on the Copper River), which NLDI does not serve. The NWM Alaska domain covers Cook Inlet and the Copper River only. Transboundary: a river whose channel crosses the U.S. international boundary (U.S. Census TIGER/Line 2024); channel outside the U.S. is drawn dashed, channel that follows the boundary (border rivers) dotted.
